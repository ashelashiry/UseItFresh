03 What the flags mean

Four things worth deciding on while the screens are open. Each is a real behaviour in the running app, found by driving it rather than reading the code.

Storage locations shows nothing

The household has three locations in the database — Fridge, Freezer and Pantry, created automatically when the household was — but the screen lists none of them. Either the query is not running or it is not filtered to the right household.

"Manage household" offers to create one

It opens the same setup screen a new person sees, so someone who already has a household is shown only "Create a household" and "Join with an invite code". It should show the household they are in: its name, who is in it, and the invite code to share.

"Set up your profile" opens empty

The name field does not load the name already saved. Anyone returning to change their units has to retype their name or lose it.

Filled buttons are hard to read

White text on the dark theme's primary green is about 2.4:1 — under the 4.5:1 that accessibility guidance asks of body text, and visibly soft on a phone outdoors. Dark text on that same green, or a deeper green behind the white, both clear it.
