Free and Plus

What each plan includes, how the app locks Plus features, how a plan is set, and what is still to decide. Built on main on 15 Sep 2026 (snapshot feat-plus-15sep).

Promise of Plus: meals that fit your goals, made with food you already have.

One Plus for the household

When anyone in a household has Plus, everyone in it gets Plus. Meal plans, receipts and recipes are shared, so a half-locked household would not work.

Free stays genuinely useful

Your kitchen, date reminders, allergy exclusions and meal ideas are free for good. Nobody is nagged to waste less.

Only the server sets a plan

The app can read a plan but never change one. Today plans are set by hand for testing; later a purchase sets them.

How a plan is set

  • Each account has profiles.plan (free or plus), plan_source (test today; later app_store) and plan_expires_at (empty means no end). Added by migration 10, 20260915150000_plans_free_plus.sql.
  • A database trigger keeps those three columns unchanged when the app updates a profile, so nobody can make themselves Plus. The SQL editor and server functions can change them.
  • The app treats you as Plus when your own plan is Plus and not expired, or anyone in your current household is. It rechecks whenever Home or Profile opens.
  • If Plus ends, nothing is deleted: plans, receipts and recipes stay in the database and come back when Plus does. Free accounts just see the lock.
  • Before migration 10 is run, every account is open (nothing locked).
