What to try in the new build

The new things, in the order I’d try them. Each photo costs a little of your Gemini credit.

  • Scan → Receipt Photograph a real till receipt, flat and in good light. Check the names and where each food will go. Tap a line to fix a name, tap ✕ to remove one, then tap Add them to your kitchen.
  • Scan → Fridge photo Photograph a shelf. It lists only what it can actually see.
  • Add manually → photo Take a photo of one food. The name and category fill in, marked as a suggestion. It never overwrites what you typed.
  • Scan → Barcode Scan a product. Its photo now stays even if the product database changes it later.
  • Profile → Reminders Turn them on with 1 day before, then add something dated two days from now. The reminder arrives tomorrow at 10am.
  • Airplane mode With no signal, open the kitchen, then an item, then Add food. Each says it can’t reach your kitchen instead of pretending. Turn the signal back on and tap Try again. (Kitchen: Build 4. Item and Add food: Build 5.)
  • Home “Use these next” appears once something is close to its date. Until then Home shows your food, and a plain plate for anything without a photo.
