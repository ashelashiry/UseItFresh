Food pictures

The {{COUNT}} photos the app can show instead of a scan when "Fresh photos first" is on. Type a food as it appears in the app to see which photo it gets. Tinned, frozen and jarred foods only get a photo of that form.

Photos are generated illustrations, not brand products. The app downloads the matching rules from design/library/index.json each time it runs, so photos added there show on phones without a new build.
